## Leaderboard Modal (scope x view)

The leaderboard modal (600px, `leaderboard-modal-content`) is a Daily/Weekly SCOPE pill-toggle crossed with three VIEW tabs (`data-lb-view`): **Scores** (the classic table, top-3 rank coloring, an "N played" count in the date line), **Adjusted** (the DEFAULT view, both scopes, `time / k` where `k` is the player's multiplicative handicap ratio from the SHIPPED handicaps.json, via `rankAdjusted` in `src/logic/leaderboardViews.js`; ranks by fractional performance vs one's own par. Daily ranks today's times; Weekly ranks the week's `bestTime` by the same dimensionless ratio (the fit is daily-anchored, so weekly leans on the ratio generalizing across boards). The HC chip is a RATING vs Greg, `(1−k)×refPar` seconds AND `(1−k)×100`%, positive = FASTER/better (`ratioDisplaySeconds`/`ratioDisplayPercent`). Players not in the fit rank by raw time (`k=1`) with an `unrated` chip; client-side provisional handicaps are self-only and never applied to others), and **Friends** (friend-code block + add-by-code + list with two-tap remove, above a scores table filtered to friends ∪ me via `filterToFriends`). Friends I/O lives in `src/firebase/firebaseFriends.js` (lazy-imported); pure logic in `src/logic/friendCodes.js` + `src/logic/leaderboardViews.js` (node-tested). `fetchOnlineLeaderboard` rows carry `uid` for the Adjusted/Friends views.


## Daily History Chart
- `src/ui/dailyHistoryChart.js`, pure-SVG timeline of the signed-in user's past 30 days of deltas from par (lives on the Stats modal's Daily tab; a play-day column can show several dots, a live daily plus archive replays). The stats tab's percentile-trend chart is toggleable Adjusted/Raw (Adjusted default: each player's best time / their shipped `k` via `rankAdjusted`). No external library. Dots colored green (under par) / gray (even) / red (over).
- Data source: `users/{uid}/dailyHistory/{date} = { time, submittedAt, archive? }` in Firebase, ONLY the raw time is stored. Timeline placement is by the day the run was PLAYED (`attributePlayedDate` in archiveEligibility.js, surfaced as `playedDate` by `fetchUserDailyHistory`): live rows belong to their board date (even when the write flushed late), archive replays to their submittedAt ET day, so a replay never back-dates a dot or pollutes the career/rolling averages, while par/features still join on the BOARD date (`boardDate` in statsRenderer). When a live play and a replay share a day, the live play owns the chart dot. Par and delta are computed at render time against the current `PAR_MODEL` (via `predictPar` on `dailyMeta/{date}` features) combined with the user's current ratio handicap. This keeps older entries automatically in sync with the latest model after each refit, no server-side rewrites needed.
- The delta plotted is against the user's PERSONAL par (`global par × k + bombSeconds`), so a dot below zero means "better than your typical" regardless of how fast you are in absolute terms.
