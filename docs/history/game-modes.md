## Game Modes

**The ladder is "The Climb" to a PLAYER and "challenge" to the code (2026-08-10).** The word "Challenge" is reserved for the head-to-head mode; every player-visible string that meant the ladder now says The Climb (or "Climb Level N"). Nothing STORED moved and nothing may: the runtime mode value is `'normal'`, the storage name is `'challenge'`, and the module filenames (`challenge250.js` et al.) are coupled to sw.js precache paths. Two tests hold the line from opposite sides, `test/challengeStorageContract.test.mjs` requires the nine stored things to keep the name, and `test/climbCopy.test.mjs` forbids the word in player-visible copy (scanning index.html's visible text plus every `src/**/*.js`, with a `SANCTIONED_PHRASES` hook for the head-to-head mode's own copy when it lands). A find-and-replace across the repo therefore turns one of them red rather than turning up in production.
- **The Climb (`gameMode: 'normal'`, stored as `challenge`):** the Challenge 250 ladder, 250 authored levels in 50 blocks of 5; a level is a PAR RATING (spec = tier × shape × modifier set) and every attempt draws a fresh certified frozen board with the marked Start-here opener. Checkpoints every 5 (block = survival unit; death returns to block start); classic loss + lifelines, never strikes; modifiers from L6 (walls intro block); all seven shapes from block 6 on.
- **Timed:** Race the clock, 4 difficulty tabs (Beginner/Intermediate/Expert/Extreme). Every timed board gets a par at generation (same PAR_MODEL as daily; gimmick terms zero on gimmick-free boards) shown as a par-relative delta on the win modal; wins submit {name, time, level, uid, par, features} to timed/{pushId} (features embedded per row - every timed board is unique, no meta bucket to join). Quick play has its OWN equation, PAR_MODEL_TIMED (between TIMED_PAR_MODEL markers in difficulty.js, refit-owned): timed rows exist only on WINS (losses never report), so the sample is win-censored and is never pooled with daily completions. The refit fits it as par-for-a-winning-run on the LOG scale (like the daily model, `scale:'log'`), on ratio-adjusted times (`log(time / k)`, dividing out the player's multiplicative handicap) with priors centered on the daily log posterior and a two-tailed outlier screen ([0.3x, 3x] predicted - the slow tail catches AFK rows); below TIMED_FIT_THRESHOLD=20 usable rows it ships as a verbatim copy of PAR_MODEL. predictPar selects the model on features.modeTimed (stamped by the timed path).
- **Daily:** One seeded puzzle per day, no levels. Modifiers are force-injected on improvement days when the target maps to a gimmick (sonar, wormhole, walls, mystery, liar, locked, mirror, compass, worm), see Adaptive Experimental Design. Falls back to the natural ~35% lottery only when the target is a non-gimmick feature (move-type counts, structural features). The SHAPE ROTATION (50% square / 50% one of the six tilings, dark behind `TILING_ROTATION_START = null`) lives in `src/logic/shapeRotation.js`, see the Board Topology section. Past dailies are replayable via the **Daily Archive** (a "Past dailies" link on the Daily card opens a month calendar), see that section.
- **Weekly:** ONE single canonical puzzle for the whole ET week (Monday → Sunday), one attempt per day for 7 days. Best time across the 7 attempts wins. Same bomb-hit mechanic as daily (info-value penalty: `+infoValue + BOMB_PENALTY_BASE × (1 + BOMB_PENALTY_RAMP × (strikeNumber − 1))`, the base RAMPS gently per hit (`strikeNumber = priorHits + 1`, `BOMB_PENALTY_RAMP = 0.5` in `handleDailyBombHit`, so 1st +3s, 2nd +4.5s, 3rd +6s, 4th +7.5s …; first hit unchanged, each later one adds half a base, discourages casual mine-popping without clobbering a legit multi-hit day, since the anti-cheat gate below handles brute-force; softened 2026-06-16 from a steeper × n ramp), where info-value is the par-seconds of deduction the struck mine was anchoring, computed by `computeBombInfoValue` via a double solver run, see `src/logic/bombInfoValue.js`. NO re-fog: every other revealed cell stays revealed; the mine is preserved so adjacent numbers don't drop. The hit cell stays revealed with `cell.isStrike = true` so the strike sprite marks where the bomb was; strike cells count as flags for chording, persist across subsequent hits, AND subtract from the LCD mine counter (`mineCounterRemaining` in headerRenderer, board-derived from `cell.isStrike`, mode-blind: the old per-mode bombHits read was gated on daily/weekly, so Par Lab strikes never reached the display and the counter sat stuck, useless as an endgame counting clue, his report 2026-08-02; pinned in `test/remainingSafe.test.mjs`). A chord that exposes SEVERAL mines (two wrong flags around a satisfied number) charges EVERY one as its own strike in one batch, per-mine marginal info-value + ramped base, one shared popup (`handleDailyBombHit(row, col, extraMines)`; pinned in `test/chordPerMine.test.mjs`); challenge/timed keep the re-fog (`unrevealChordMines`) since one lifeline/loss handles the first mine there. Penalties live in the `bombHitEvents` log, not in wall-clock `elapsedTime`. Popup labels Minor/Key/Critical mines at info-value ≥ 2 / 6.5 / 13, quantile-anchored by `scripts/reanchor-bomb-tiers.mjs`. No game-over. **Anti-cheat:** a run that finds most of the board's mines by stepping on them is probing the layout, not playing, `isBombHitCheat` (difficulty.js) flags it and the submit gates in `submitOnlineScore` / `submitWeeklyScore` / `submitArchiveScore` reject it (returns `'cheat'` / `false`, never queued for retry), so it never reaches the leaderboard or the par fit. The player still finishes, and **the DAY still counts**, streak, completion and the `dailyHistory` row are all written, the same ruling the `'divergent'` branch carries ("I don't want people losing their streak, but I also don't want bad data"). **TWO arms, because a bare fraction is scale-FREE and the board scale moved under it** (2026-08-09): flagged when `hits > max(BOMB_HIT_CHEAT_FLOOR (10), BOMB_HIT_CHEAT_FRACTION (0.50) × mines)` OR `hits >= BOMB_HIT_EXCAVATED_FRACTION (0.80) × mines`. The first arm is "far more mistakes than a bad day", floored because half of a 6-mine board proves nothing; the second is "you excavated the board", and it is the ONLY arm that can fire where the floor exceeds the mine count, so without it every board of ≤10 mines would be ungated even against a total excavation. **The old flat 30% was written against the 25-30-mine rectangles of the time, where it means nine mistakes**, and the shape rotation ships `TILING_BAND_CONFIGS` entries with as few as **6 mines**, where the same 30% means TWO. It refused Kate at 3 hits on the 9-mine Kites daily of 2026-08-09, an ordinary bad day on the dearest lattice we ship, and cost her the leaderboard row and (before this) the dot on her own history chart. **Calibrated on every row ever submitted**: 602 per-attempt readings across daily / archive / weekly, worst GENUINE run 25% of the mines (Kate, 7/28 on 2026-05-01, two hits from refusal under the old rule), the three real probing episodes at 81 / 100 / 100%. The two populations never come near each other, and loosening to these numbers changes **ZERO historical verdicts**, the same four readings are refused before and after, so the par fit is byte-identical. Note the observed 25% is **CENSORED by this very gate** (a refused run was never written, so the true right tail of honest play is unobservable, which is exactly what happened to Kate), which is why the floor sits at 10 rather than the 8 the visible data alone would put it. Mirrored in `refit-par-model.R`, `backfit-model-history.R`, `par-experiment.qmd`, `intercept-sensitivity.qmd` and `fit-parlab-priors.qmd`, **all six copies must move together**; pinned in `test/bomb-penalty.test.mjs`.) Board size 8-14 × 8-14, 2-4 modifiers stacked, daily-density. Architecture mirrors daily's canonical-board: `weeklyBoard/{weekStart}` is write-once on Firebase, pre-generated by `precompute-weekly-board.yml` Monday 00:00 UTC (`scripts/precompute-weekly-board.mjs`). Cloud-synced one-attempt-per-day cap at `users/{uid}/weeklyAttempts/{weekStart}/dayAttempts/{N}` so clearing localStorage can't grant a second try. **The cap refuses a SECOND attempt; RESUMING the first is not one** (issue #246). The attempt is committed on the FIRST CLICK, so a mine hit followed by a restart cannot buy another, but the mode card refused entry on that same marker, and since it is the only production caller of `switchMode('weekly')`, the resume branch behind it could never run: one Home tap mid-attempt and the board was unreachable for good, the day spent on a puzzle nobody finished. All three doors (mode card, `?mode=weekly`, Play Again) now route through the pure `weeklyEntryPlan` in resumeEligibility.js, `attempted` from the marker, `resumable` from `canResumeMode('weekly')`, which reads the same slot against the same clock context `tryResumeGame` uses. A FINISHED attempt reports `resumable: false` because winning clears the slot, so the block still fires exactly where it should. Gathered once in `weeklyEntryContext` (main.js) so the three doors cannot drift; pinned in `test/resumeEligibility.test.mjs`, including a source scan that no door regrows a private marker check. Note the e2e harness CANNOT reach this class: `markWeeklyDayAttempted` is a no-op under `isTestEnvironment()`, so the gate never arms there. Leaderboard is one row per player per week at `weekly/{weekStart}/{uid}` (set, not push) carrying `bestTime` and a `dayTimes` map. Submission helpers: `submitWeeklyScore`/`fetchWeeklyLeaderboard` (firebaseLeaderboard.js), `markWeeklyDayAttempted`/`loadWeeklyAttempts` (firebaseProgress.js). Seed selection in `selectWeeklyRngSeed.js` scores 10 candidates by `activeGimmicks.length + advancedLogicMoves * 0.01`, weighted toward the week's target par by the banded argmax in `parBand.js` (weekly band 60-360s, see Par Bands; the precompute calls this same function, ONE copy). Gimmick pool: `getWeeklyGimmicks` picks 2-4 distinct from the daily-safe subset, no `singleOnly`. **First-attempt-of-the-week qualifies for par-model fit data, LIVE since 2026-08-04, and it never was before.** The submission has existed since v1.6 behind `WEEKLY_FIT_DATA_ENABLED`, set false as a deliberate hold while the weekly's rules were still moving; the flag outlived its reason and was never flipped, so `daily/{weekStart}_weekly_first` was EMPTY for all fourteen weeks with leaderboard rows. The symptom was silent because the PRECOMPUTE writes the matching `dailyMeta/{weekStart}_weekly_first` at generation and only the SCORE half was gated, meta present, scores absent, nothing failing. `scripts/backfill-weekly-fit-rows.mjs` (+ its dispatch workflow) recovered all 34 player-weeks: each player's first played day comes out of the day-indexed `dayTimes`/`dayBombHits` (holes for unplayed days, and Firebase returns a sparse array as an OBJECT, so both shapes are read), and features are recomputed from the stored canonical. It deliberately includes NO invented `bombHitEvents`/`wormEvents`, those were never recorded per attempt, so the rows join the refit's existing legacy-bomb path rather than faking precision, and is marked `backfilled: true` (whitelisted in the rules) so the fit can always tell them apart. Mechanism: when `Object.keys(state.weeklyDayTimes).length === 0` at win time, winLossHandler additionally calls `submitOnlineScore('{weekStart}_weekly_first', ...)` so the score lands in `daily/{weekStart}_weekly_first` and features in `dailyMeta/{weekStart}_weekly_first`. Days 2-7 attempts are speedruns of a known board and stay out of the fit. Weekly does NOT touch the DAILY streak / handicap / `dailyHistory`, it has its own WEEK streak (below). The Stats modal has a Weekly tab with a 7-day line chart.
- **The Weekly card gets the Daily card's furniture (2026-08-05, v1.11): a week streak, a par, and Past weeklies.** His ask, and the shape of it is the daily's: numbers in the CORNERS so the center line stays one descriptor and the card stays its siblings' height. Week streak bottom-left, par bottom-right, the Past chip top-right; the top-LEFT corner stays deliberately EMPTY, because that is the daily's molt slot and **the weekly has no molt days** (his call: a week is already seven chances at one board, so the insurance would be insuring the window that IS the insurance).
  - **The week streak: ONE completion banks the week** (his rule, "only need to play one of the weekly to get a week streak"), so the seven attempts are the week's generosity rather than seven obligations, and finishing on Tuesday and again on Friday is the same week. COMPLETION banks it, not merely opening a board, the same bar the daily uses, and the weekly has no loss state, so "played" would otherwise be one click on a board nobody solved. Consecutive weeks build it; any wider gap restarts at 1; `best` is a high-water mark that never lapses. A streak is ALIVE through the week after its last completion (this week is still running, so nothing is broken yet) and reads 0 after that, a read-side view, never a rewrite. Math in the pure `src/logic/weeklyProgress.js` (`applyWeekContinuation` / `isWeekStreakAlive` / `projectWeekContinuation` / `liveWeekStreak`), storage in `statsStorage` as a TOP-LEVEL `stats.weekStreak = {streak, best, lastWeek}`, deliberately not `modeStats.weekly`, which does not exist, because creating it would silently start routing every weekly completion through `saveGameResult`'s per-mode counters. Cloud-synced as ONE object at `users/{uid}/weekStreak` (the moltDay atomic-snapshot lesson) and whitelisted in `firebase-rules.json`; the users block ends with `$other: false`, so the whitelist is what keeps the WHOLE progress write from dropping (the 866683d class), pinned in both directions by `test/weekStreak.test.mjs`. Merge rules mirror the daily's on dates: newer week adopted verbatim (even a SHORTER streak, the other device saw the break), same week adopts the longer, older keeps local, and a node with no `lastWeek` says nothing rather than zeroing. **The self-heal PUSHES its correction** (issue #248): `reconcileWeekStreakFromHistory` wrote only localStorage, and nothing but a completion writes this node, so for the very players the backfill exists for, long history, no post-ship completion yet, the cloud kept the value the heal had just disagreed with, and the progress listener re-applies the cloud VERBATIM on every write under `users/{uid}` (a `lastSeen` beacon, a daily completion, another device). The card was raised at boot and knocked back down for the rest of the session, and a completion landing on the low side banked it. Both self-heals now write back: `getWeekStreakRecord()` / `getDailyCloudSnapshot()` are the ONE definition of each node's payload shape (read back from storage, never re-assembled at the call site, the completion path reads the same accessor), and both push AFTER `applyCloudProgress` has merged the cloud in, so what goes up is cloud-merged then corrected rather than a stale local value overwriting a fresher remote one. `getWeekStreakRecord` deliberately returns the STORED streak, not `getWeekStreak`'s lapse-adjusted view: a lapse is a read-side 0, and writing it would make it permanent for every device. **The heal derives from BOTH per-week records now, split at a stated epoch, which closes the two halves #254 left open.** `users/{uid}/weeklyCompletions/{weekStart}` is the per-week COMPLETION record the weekly always lacked: the win path writes it via `markWeeklyCompleted` (firebaseProgress, internally test-gated like the attempt marker) on the week's FIRST completion only, gated on `recordWeeklyCompletion`'s `extended` flag with a write-once rule behind it, payload `{timestamp}` (server sentinel; `backfilled: true` is reserved for the recovery script), whitelist lockstep pinned in both directions by `test/weeklyCompletions.test.mjs` (the 866683d class). The heal's derivation is `bankableWeeks` (weeklyProgress.js): weeks before `WEEKLY_COMPLETIONS_EPOCH` ('2026-08-10', FROZEN in both directions, moving it earlier claims records that were never written and moving it later re-admits the #254 class) keep the documented attempts generosity, since attempts are the only per-week record of that era, while from the epoch on an attempt alone banks NOTHING, so an opened-and-abandoned week stops counting once its era is covered by the record. Both sources route through `streakBearingWeeks`, unchanged and still fail-CLOSED without a `currentWeek` (this module has no clock by design), so the live-week exclusion covers completions exactly as it covers attempts (#254 itself: an abandoned attempt on the LIVE week banked it, spliced it onto a genuine run, measured: a real 2-week run read 3, and raised the monotonic `best`, which nothing can lower); `reconcileWeekStreakFromHistory(attempted, currentWeek, completed)` stays upward-only, which is also what makes a dropped completion write harmless to the streak itself, since the trio is written at the same win. `weekArchiveState`'s set comes from `fetchCompletedWeeks` now, so `'done'` means FINISHED and an attempted-but-abandoned past week is tappable again (the board was never cleared; there is something left to find). History for NAMED players is seeded by `scripts/backfill-weekly-completions.mjs` (+ dispatch workflow, dry-run default): every `weekly/{weekStart}/{uid}` row proves a completion (the rules require `bestTime`, which only a win writes), entries are skip-if-existing so a re-run never replaces a live first-completion stamp, and re-running is the intended way to catch wins from stale clients that predate the record. A NAMELESS player's pre-epoch completions have no evidence anywhere and stay unrecorded, failing OPEN on every surface: those archive weeks read playable (a replay records nothing) and the streak keeps its generous pre-epoch derivation.
  - **Par on the card** comes from `computeWeeklyPar` (parResolve), which solves the week's canonical once per session. There is no cheap local path like the daily's cached features, the weekly's board exists only on Firebase, so `refreshTitleWeeklyPar` REFUSES to start when `state.firebaseReady` is false rather than opening a poll that cannot land, and the corner just goes missing until a later title visit. The weekly still shows no par at the END of a run: days 2-7 are speedruns of a known board, and rating a memorised solve against a first-encounter par means nothing. On the card it is the opposite question, how big the waiting board is, before you spend one of seven attempts on it.
  - **Past weeklies** (`#weekly-archive-modal`, a LIST not a calendar, one board per week, so a month grid would be six empty cells around the row that matters). `weeklyProgress.js` owns the window: `FIRST_ARCHIVE_WEEK = '2026-05-04'` (the mode's launch; there is no backfill, because regenerating a past week from its seed would be a board nobody played) through last week, current week excluded as the live Weekly's job. `launchWeeklyArchive` (modeManager) mirrors `launchDailyArchive` exactly, and `state.isWeeklyArchive` walls the replay off: the caller-set PAST weekStart survives newGame's clock derivation, `weeklyDay` is NULL (it belongs to no day and consumes no attempt), the board comes from `_weeklyArchiveRaw` verbatim and never touches the live stash, there is NO local-gen fallback, it never persists (it shares the weekly save slot), and the whole win block, attempt marking, leaderboard row, first-attempt fit row, week streak, is skipped. Which weeks are marked `'done'` comes from `fetchCompletedWeeks` reading the player's OWN `users/{uid}/weeklyCompletions` keys, never from `weeklyAttempts` (an attempt is a first click, and marking done from attempts locked abandoned weeks out of their own replay) and not the shared `weekly/` leaderboard (no uid index, it grows with every player, and a nameless player's completions never reach it); unknown history fails OPEN, like the daily calendar's. The in-game header names the week instead of a weekday so a replay can never read as this week's attempt.
  - **An archive replay never EXPIRES on a clock wake** (`isLiveGameExpired` now returns false for `isArchivePlay` / `isWeeklyArchive`). Expiry exists so yesterday's unfinished attempt cannot resurrect as today's; a replay is not an attempt at anything, and before this, backgrounding the tab during one and returning expired the board mid-play with a "New day!" toast. The DAILY archive had the same hole and is fixed in the same line.
- **Chaos:** Rapid rounds with random modifiers, exempt from solvability guarantee. **The board's SHAPE rolls per round since 2026-08-04** (`src/logic/chaosShape.js`, pure): the round rolls its own shape, ~50% lattice, and the round's rectangular size is TRANSLATED rather than re-authored, `getChaosDifficulty` ramps a side length and a density, and a lattice has no side length, so the round's CELL COUNT is the currency and each shape picks the (M, N) landing nearest it at the same density. Three things are specific to this mode and none of them applies anywhere else in the game:
  - **The board is a lattice BEFORE the first click.** Chaos generates its mines FROM the click, so the placeholder `createEmptyBoard` is stamped with the real topology at newGame and the mines go into it afterwards. That is also why `generateTilingBoard` gained `openerIndex`: every other tiling caller opens on the patch center, and chaos must open where the player actually clicked or the first click could be a mine.
  - **Two lattices are excluded and it is measured, not aesthetic.** Rhombille and deltoidal both stall: rhombille's certifier has no Pass B and leans on Pass C for every board, and deltoidal measured 3.7s on a round-12 board, against ~1.1s worst for the four that ship. Chaos is the ONE place a multi-second generation is unacceptable, because it happens under a click with the clock about to start; both stay on the authored ladder and in the endless pool, where generation hides behind a level card. Sub-0.22-density plans route `forceConstructive` for the same reason a miss there is a failed click rather than a slow one.
  - **`state.totalMines` is reconciled with the board, not the plan.** The generator places `min(mines, placeable)` since the opener and its neighbors are excluded, so the requested count is a ceiling; the LCD counter is board-derived and the win check counts safe cells, so state has to agree with the board. Pinned in `test/chaosShape.test.mjs` (shape roll, size translation, ribbon guard, a generation-budget check) and `tests/e2e/chaosShape.spec.mjs` (the lattice is on screen pre-click and the click opens ground; break-tested by forcing the roll to zero). Quick Play stays rectangular.
