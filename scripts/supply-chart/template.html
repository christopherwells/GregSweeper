Half of "cannot be built" was the pool's reach, not physics

Yesterday's conclusion was that the empty corners could not exist under absolute cutoffs. His answer was that generation might need to be rethought, and the probes proved him right about half of it. The pool's size ladder stops far short of what the fit rules allow: rect's legal ceiling is 17x11 = 187 cells against the pool's legal 143, and in that unsearched range long-standard rectangles certify plain at 329s. Eight of the sixteen hard cells opened in one night, two of them with no modifiers at all. Generation now searches to the fit rules themselves: candidates are fit-checked at consumption, any board already in a cell's mix donates its geometry, and each shape's legal big end is synthesized rather than hoped for.

The other half is physics with measured ceilings now: hex sparse tops out at 99s at every legal size, 4.8.8 long-sparse at 128s, rect long-sparse at 174s on 187 cells wearing five modifiers, and rhombille's sparse cells sit near 51s. The sparse question is about exactly that residue, and nothing else: a handful of cells where "long" cannot mean four minutes for this shape at this density, whatever generation does.

Two ways to count a modifier, and the narrow one is the honest floor

The deal accepts a board when every modifier on it is one the host permitted. So a host who allows sonar and nothing else draws only plain boards and sonar boards, while a host who allows everything draws all 336 boards in the Petals sparse quick column. Any set containing it counts the second player's shelf; only this modifier counts the first player's, which is the floor under every wider choice.

The two disagree by more than they look, and the eviction sharpened the gap. Pooled over the match library alone, 41 cells hold nothing: the physics residue, the cells the legality pass emptied, and band membership moving under the new model, summed. Narrow reads 66; the union with the Climb's boards leaves 29 narrow empties and exactly one cell with nothing at all. The single-modifier holes the narrow view first exposed were filled where boards can exist, deltoidal, floret and rhombille each carrying single-modifier stock in their once-stacked cells now, and the rest were reclassified by the probes rather than left as promises.

Decided

Objective
Score each candidate as supply need × model leverage, so a board that fills a thin corner and extends the fit wins outright. No fixed split to tune.
Hard corners
Exponential backoff with a record. A corner that resists is tried less often, never abandoned, and tonight's run reads what last night learned.
Per board
Store a durable leverage score: the board's distance from the rest of the library in feature space. Mission fit stays computed at deal time, because the mission changes nightly.
Budget
Ladder first, match takes the remainder of the 20 minutes. The ladder is satisfied today (deficits.json is empty), so the remainder is the whole budget.
One shelf
The two libraries become a library for all. The ladder draws from it before generating anything; Challenge plays the same boards under a different clock, where time counts and mines do not end the run.
Seen-sets
Separate. A Climb play does not spend a board for Challenge: nobody carries a memory of one board across that many plays, and the two modes reward different things.
Digit priors
Settled by measurement, not argument. The shape indicators inherit the Par Lab centers; re-fitting with everything zero-centered moved the four digit shares by at most 0.08 SD, and clueShare3 by 0.00. The inheritance stays.

    How to read it. Each panel is one density band crossed with one length band. Rows are shapes, columns are modifiers, and the number is boards nobody has finished yet.

    The columns deliberately do not sum to the library total. Under any set containing it a board counts once for each of its modifiers, so a two-modifier board counts twice. The deal filters on the modifier set, not on single modifiers, which is why the match library's 1,290 true corners collapse into this grid's 513 occupied cells.

    Climb boards count as unplayed here even when somebody has finished them on the ladder. Challenge runs the same board under a different clock, where time counts and a mine does not end the run, so a ladder play does not spend it.
